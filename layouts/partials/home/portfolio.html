About me

I’m Daniel Halawi, a full-stack developer who enjoys turning practical problems into clear, dependable products. I care equally about the experience people see and the systems working underneath it.

My curiosity started with taking computers apart, experimenting with Linux, and learning why things break. That hands-on mindset still shapes the way I work: understand the problem, remove unnecessary complexity, and build something people can trust.

I’m especially interested in full-stack systems, thoughtful interfaces, and practical uses of AI that make software more useful without making it harder to understand.
